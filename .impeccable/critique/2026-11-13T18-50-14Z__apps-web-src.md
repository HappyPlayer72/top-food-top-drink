### Overall Impression

The mobile editorial ranking list (`topfood` and `topdrink` at `375px` and `320px`) is cohesive, readable, and keeps `scrollWidth === innerWidth` with zero page-level horizontal overflow. However, the app stops short of being genuinely mobile-usable on its interactive workflows: `Hartă` breaks down under dense Piața Unirii marker overlap, z-index marker bleed over the filter sheet, and a zero-result-rows sheet on `320×568`; dark-mode sheets render a near-invisible close button; bottom sheets look swipeable without swipe logic or focus trapping; and onboarding/bookmarking both break user promises.

### What's Working

1. **Cohesive Dual-World Brand Worlds**: `topfood` and `topdrink` keep distinct color/score metaphors (`Carou` vs `Sticlă` & `Coaster`), legible condensed scores (`tabular-nums`), and clear organic vs sponsored separation on mobile cards.
2. **Solid Core List Ergonomics (`375px` & `320px`)**: `16px` form inputs prevent iOS auto-zoom, header `Cont` and bottom navigation (`h-14`) stay unclipped down to `320px`, and `MonthlyTopView` segmented tabs (`Localuri | Recenzenți | Angajați`) fit cleanly in a single `>= 44px` bar.
3. **Skeptic-Friendly Transparency**: Explicit `Localnici vs Turiști` splits, `"De ce?"` trap explanations, and `"Sponsorizat. Nu schimbă scorul."` build real product credibility.

### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Selected Map Pin Bleeds Over `Listă și filtre` Sheet, Overlapping Piața Unirii Pins, and Zero Venue Rows Visible at `320×568`**
  - **Why it matters**: In `MapView.tsx`, an active MapLibre pin wrapper (`zIndex: '30'`) sits later in DOM stacking than `<aside>` (`z-30`), causing the selected pin (`9,1 Ciorba lui Sandu`) to paint directly over the open bottom sheet's rows. At initial zoom `13.5`, Piața Unirii pins overlap (`text-occlusion` 100% on `7,9`, `5,8`, `8,6`) under crowded `10px` yellow `300 m` zone pills (`107×26px`), and at `320×568` the sheet's stacked filter controls + 3-line compass legend fill all `391px` height so **0 venue rows** are visible without scrolling.
  - **Fix**: Elevate mobile `<aside>` to `z-40` and wrap the map canvas in an isolated `z-10` stacking context; cluster or offset dense center pins below zoom `15`; raise pin/zone hitboxes to `44×44px` and `>= 11px` labels; and collapse/condense mobile sheet filter chrome into a compact horizontal row so at least 2 venue rows appear immediately on `320px`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout` (and `/impeccable harden`)

- **[P0] Invisible Close (`×`) in Dark-Mode `ReviewDrawer` & Low-Contrast Dark-Mode Map Filter Cards**
  - **Why it matters**: `globals.css` dark-mode overrides skip Tailwind opacity-modified selectors (`text-[#3A2418]/80`, `bg-[#E0A526]/20`, `bg-[#B3261E]/12`), leaving `ReviewDrawer`'s `×` dismiss icon in `RestaurantDetailModal.tsx` at ~1.2:1 dark-brown-on-charcoal contrast on a drawer that also lacks swipe-to-dismiss, while dark-mode `Mixt` and `▲ Capcană` cards in `MapView.tsx` wash out. Also `topdrink` `#3` podium text falls slightly short (`4.2:1`, `#17382c` on `#b98a5e`), and `#food-search-input` placeholder is `4.1:1`.
  - **Fix**: Replace brittle raw-hex dark overrides in `globals.css` with semantic theme CSS custom properties (including explicit dark contrast variants for close buttons, tier cards, and `topdrink` `#3` podium card `#17382c` -> `#F4EEE2` or lighter bronze `#C99D73` and darker placeholder ink).
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit` (and `/impeccable polish`)

- **[P1] Misleading `2,0×` UVT/UPT Academic Email Badge Contradicts Actual Account Role Assignment**
  - **Why it matters**: In `LoginScreen.tsx`, typing an `@e-uvt.ro` / `@upt.ro` email shows `Domeniu UVT / UPT (2,0×)` (even inside `Intră în cont` login mode), and the explainer explicitly states UVT/UPT emails grant `2,0×`. Yet submitting `Cont nou` creates a `1,0×` (`tourist`) session unless the user also toggles `"Simulează check-in GPS"`.
  - **Fix**: Honor `@e-uvt.ro` / `@upt.ro` in `isVerifiedLocal` (or remove `(2,0×)` from the email badge and explainer), restrict the badge to `mode === 'register'`, and replace `"Simulează check-in GPS"` jargon with realistic verification UI.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify` (and `/impeccable harden`)

- **[P1] Guest Bookmarks Have No UI Surface or Persistence + Tab/Auth Navigation Wipes List Scroll**
  - **Why it matters**: Guests can tap `Salvează localul` inside `RestaurantDetailModal`, see `"Salvat: ..."`, and then have zero Saved UI in `Navbar.tsx` (gated to `currentUser !== null`) while losing all saved IDs on reload. Worse, `TopFoodShell.tsx` forces `window.scrollTo({ top: 0 })` when backing out of login or switching sections, wiping reading position, and the toast (`z-[60]`) overlaps the modal's bottom sticky bar.
  - **Fix**: Persist `savedIds` to `localStorage` and expose saved venues to guests (or prompt sign-in before saving), preserve per-tab `scrollY` instead of forcing `scrollTo({ top: 0 })`, and offset toast `bottom` when modals are open.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

- **[P1] Non-Functional Bottom-Sheet Drag Handles, Missing Modal Focus Traps & Sub-`44px` Touch Targets**
  - **Why it matters**: All mobile bottom sheets (`Navbar` Profile, `EditorialListView` Vibe Check, `RestaurantDetailModal` `ReviewDrawer`, `MapView` filters) display `w-10 h-1` drag pills with zero swipe-to-dismiss handlers, and `aria-modal="true"` overlays lack keyboard focus traps / restoration (`Tab` exits behind sheets; `RestaurantDetailModal` `min-h-full` prevents backdrop tap close). Live telemetry measured `17/29` interactive elements `< 44×44px` on `Hartă` (`26px` zone pills, `40px` camera chips/pins/sheet buttons), `28px` brand button height, and `36px`/`24px` touch slider thumb on `#rev-score-range`.
  - **Fix**: Unify mobile drawers/modals in one accessible bottom-sheet wrapper with `Tab` focus trapping, backdrop dismiss, and vertical drag-to-close; enforce `min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px]` wrappers across mobile controls, slider thumbs, and map pins.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt` (and `/impeccable animate`)

- **[P2] Price Clipping at `375px`, Missing Horizontal Scroll Affordances, Double Select Chevron & Unfiltered Trap Column**
  - **Why it matters**: On `#1 Ciorba lui Sandu` (`375px`), the recommended dish chip clips off the price (`"...· ..."`), horizontal filter/camera rails cut labels mid-word (`"Muzică liv..."`, `"Cetate / U..."`) with no fade hint, `MonthlyTopView` renders double arrows (`Noiembrie 2026 ▼ ⌄`) on `<select>`, Next.js dev indicator (`N`) covers the bottom-left `Top` nav tab during dev testing, and searching on `topfood` leaves `Atenție în Piața Unirii` unfiltered.
  - **Fix**: Pin dish prices outside `truncate`, add CSS mask edge fades to horizontal scrollers, remove duplicate chevron overlay or apply cross-browser `appearance-none` reset cleanly, and filter `foodTraps` by `searchQuery`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`
