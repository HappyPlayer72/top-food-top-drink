### Overall Impression

The product's core idea and desktop/editorial visual voice are genuinely distinctive, and the `/owner` portal proves the team knows how to build rock-solid `44px` touch ergonomics and `16px` mobile inputs. Across the consumer mobile experience, though, readability and thumb usability break down wherever users actually interact — in the 6-tab bottom bar, the sticky detail modal footer, the map controls, loyalty QR/copy actions, and table minigames — turning a confident neighborhood guide into a fat-finger obstacle course on a `375px` phone.

### What's Working

1. **iOS-safe mobile search & primary filter pills (`EditorialListView.tsx:595, 619, 1014, 1038`):** Primary search inputs use `text-[16px] sm:text-[15px]` and `min-h-[44px]`, preventing Safari auto-zoom, while top filter chips expand to `min-h-[44px] sm:min-h-0`.
2. **Thumb-friendly saved/profile bottom sheet (`Navbar.tsx:573–612`):** Uses `max-h-[85dvh]`, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` padding, accessible `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`, a `touch-none` drag handle, and a `44×44px` (`w-11 h-11`) close button.
3. **Disciplined `/owner` mobile form system (`owner/ui-classes.ts:7–21`, `owner/ui.tsx:15–71, 184–198`):** All owner inputs enforce `min-h-11` + `text-[16px]`, buttons/chips enforce `min-h-11` + `touch-manipulation`, and `FormMessage` pairs color with icons and `aria-live="polite"`.
